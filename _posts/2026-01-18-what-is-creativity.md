It makes sense that drawing is a form of creativity. Earlier this week, I
sketched a paperclip to satisfy my urge to make something.

![A rough image of a paperclip](/assets/img/paperklip.png){: width="200"}

I suppose that should count. Working on a website feels creative, but in a
different way. There are design choices involved for sure. If the purpose of
[my experiment](../tiny-experiment/) was creative writing, then it does not
address the intent of the experiment. However, it does reveal a sense of
reluctance I faced over the past few days.
