We moved back home at the end of July. It feels like we never left. The same faces, facades, friends, and family, just older. The time away feels frivolous sometimes, like it was a false start. It wasn't. Every step toward dreams while honoring those around us was worth the cost of moving, pivoting, rebranding, and settling back into a familiar chapter at a new time in life.

My heart ached when I was away. Not too much, not too loudly, but always present enough that I could find it faster than a tongue finds the empty socket of a missing tooth. Returning here soothed that discomfort. In its place is the feeling that I could have just stayed here, could have just kept doing what I did before quitting my job and earning a degree that sometimes earns me the title "Dr."

I will not allow myself to doubt the utility of dreams and of chasing fireflies only to let them free moments later. They go on about their existences wondering what the hell happened to them. Catch and release so we can celebrate our having achieved the what-ifs of life. We made it. We captured lightning in a jar, held it for a moment with our hearts punching our ribcages, and wondered whether the act of apprehending phantasms was really our lot in life.

Yeah, I did it. I found myself at the top of the academic foodchain in a city I learned to love if not for the nagging feeling that I forfeited something to be there. I think home is where the nagging ceases. Ideally, at least. It is where we can find some sense of peace despite there being rampant imperfections. Maybe home is something that depends on contentment. Maybe I am home when I am here, where here has always been, with my whole self. I am home because I am here. And here is there or here or nowhere, dependent upon how I show up to it.
