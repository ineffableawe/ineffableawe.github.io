**My curiosity:** I want to discover whether creative writing is a thing I enjoy
or something I guilt myself into wanting to do but never quite getting around to
doing. What if I discover that I don't like it? I can stop feeling guilty about
it. And if I discover that I enjoy it, I can stop feeling guilty about it and
start doing it whenever I want to have fun.

**"Lit" review:** If I read myself, I can note that I tend to feel a sense of
burden rather than relief if I impose rules for creativity. However, I also tend
to become excited about being creative. I find that creative ideas pop up more
often when I am actively creating. Those ideas have a tendency to resound in my
head and have a way of raising my heart rate as I imagine myself sharing them.
But I don't share them because I feel like I will start strong and lose momentum
soon into the process of fleshing them out.

**"Research" question:** Will I want to continue writing after this experiment
if I show up to this website every workday for the next 10 workdays to write
something creative?

**Prediction:** I will find myself enjoying the process and will want to
continue writing.
