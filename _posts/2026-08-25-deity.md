Forgetting anything we have heard of deities, what would convince us that they exist? Is there a single piece of evidence that lacks every other explanation? Or are there fragments of truth scattered across the universe that, when combined into a cohesive whole, would support the conclusion that deities both exist and have always done so without need of others to have created them? Is some evidence stored privately throughout the cosmos behind locks that only open with faith in theretofore unsubstantiated claims of divinity?

I wonder, if all existence proceeds from some creator, why the same creator escapes from that requirement. Granting this claim for the sake of consideration, what evidence would be given in its support? Perhaps my mind lacks the requisite creativity seeing that I fail to invent such evidence. Stepping back into the only reality I have ever known, I see more plainly that the claim serves the purpose of granting special privileges to at least one being for the purposes of subordinating the rest of us. We know our place in such a universe—subservient and inferior.

We stand in awe of the imaginations we project out into the universe. The universe speaks in languages we do not comprehend, and we happy few have found an interpreter through faith. Now we understand as though looking through a darkened glass, but one day soon we will see clearly. It is the promise of this future perfect vision that entices many to hold fast to faith. But what grants the doubtful a similar view? Again my mind lacks the creativity to find evidence in the universe for any special existence beyond what I have known. I am comfortable with looking in awe at what I do not understand without attempting to explain it with another unknown. The deities have not revealed themselves plainly.

What should I do if some secret whisperings come to me and reveal that my coffee table is in truth the oldest being in the world, speaks my dialect of English through which it wishes to dictate its ordinances, and desires that I honor it with burnt offerings and daily worship? Should I check myself into a hospital, or should I buy myself a goat and two doves? Should I write down its edicts and publish them for others to learn the truth? Four will now become the preferred number of my new religion. One day, a table with three legs will rise up and claim that it was there before my table, that it is certain that four legs are accursed, and woe to those who pervert the truth by building tables of six legs, yea even five legs, for "I am the LORD, Tripodia, and you shall know me, and I shall know you, and we shall eat together in the dining room of the universe." Yes, it is absurd. But it lacks the same support in reality that other claims do.
